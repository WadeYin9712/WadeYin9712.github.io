Da Yin

I am a postdoc researcher at FAIR @ Meta Superintelligence Lab (MSL). I obtained PhD in Computer Science at UCLA, advised by Kai‑Wei Chang, and was awarded the Amazon PhD Fellowship in 2023 and 🏆 best paper award at EMNLP 2023 Pan-DL workshop.

My research focuses on building adaptive and self-evolving LLM agents.

✨ Currently I am interested in:

  • Self-Evolving LLM Agents
  • Synthetic Agent Training Data
  • LLM Agent Post-Training

  • Postdoc, FAIR @ Meta Superintelligence Lab (MSL), 2025-
  • Ph.D., University of Califorlia, Los Angeles (UCLA), 2020-2025
  • B.A., Peking University (PKU), 2016-2020
